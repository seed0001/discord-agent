Memory

Most bots hold a conversation until the context window fills, then start over as a stranger. Max keeps three things on disk and rewrites all of them after every single turn — text or voice, from anyone, in any channel — tagged with exactly where it happened.

Voice

Say the wake phrase once.

He joins the channel himself, hears every speaker as their own stream, and drops blips too short or too quiet to be speech before they cost you anything. Then for 25 seconds after he finishes, you just talk — and every real answer re-arms the window.

  • "Max, stop speaking" cuts him off mid-sentence but keeps him in the conversation.
  • "Max, stop listening" ends it and puts the wake phrase back.
  • If what he hears plainly was not meant for him, he stays quiet.

Initiative

Most drafts never send. That is the feature.

Every message and transcript is classified into typed signals — an unresolved blocker, an incorrect technical claim, a promise nobody kept, a safety concern. Each routes to its own reservoir, which charges by confidence, decays on a 30-second tick, and loses 85% of its pressure the moment he actually speaks about it.

Clearing the threshold only gets a draft written. Then it has to survive relevance, novelty, cooldown, budget and energy checks. The ones that make it through are the ones worth reading.

  • Safety concerns clear the lowest threshold and fade the fastest.
  • Pressure leaves silently when someone else solves it, or the topic is abandoned.
  • /pressure shows the whole state live, or turns it off entirely.
